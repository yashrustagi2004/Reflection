Privacy Policy

At Reflection, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our AI-powered interview preparation platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 OAuth Authentication Data

When you sign in using OAuth providers (Google or GitHub), we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Profile picture (if available)
  • OAuth provider identifier

1.2 Uploaded Documents

We process the following documents you upload:

  • Resume/CV: Personal information is automatically removed before processing
  • Job Description: Used to generate personalized interview questions

1.3 Usage Data

We may collect information about how you interact with our platform, including:

  • Questions answered and practice sessions
  • Feedback and analysis results
  • Platform usage patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized interview questions based on your resume and job description
  • Provide AI-powered analysis and feedback on your answers
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Communicate with you about your account and our services

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
  • Personal information is automatically removed from resumes before processing
  • OAuth authentication ensures your credentials are never stored on our servers
  • Regular security audits and updates

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of certain data processing activities

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use session cookies to maintain your login state. These cookies are essential for platform functionality and are automatically deleted when you log out.

7. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with OAuth providers (Google, GitHub) for authentication. Please review their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through our Contact Us page.

This Privacy Policy is effective as of December 2025.
